Frequently asked questions

What exactly is ProvenDrop?

An ecommerce market intelligence service with three components: product research and market validation, AI-powered creative production, and a collection report for every opportunity. We discover collections from generic stores already advertising on Meta and Google in international markets, validate them, and deliver each one as a Launch Kit. It is not a store, a product catalogue or an advertising agency.

Do I receive it on WhatsApp?

Yes, with the Click plan: subscribe on this page with the Click button and you’ll join the Click WhatsApp group. The standard plan delivers in the member area and by e-mail.

What time do the collections arrive?

Every day at 09:00, weekends included.

What if there is no approved collection one day?

You receive the best collections still under observation, always labelled as such, and each one comes with its Launch Kit so you can test it with a small budget. We never show a collection as approved before it has passed the next-day confirmation.

Do you ever send brands or manufacturers?

No. Every store is checked on the day it is scored: only generic (dropshipping) stores make the list. If a store shows any sign of being a brand or a manufacturer, it is excluded.

Will I get the same collection twice?

No. A collection is sent once. The only exception: a collection under observation that later passes the check is sent once more as approved.

Why Google as well as Meta?

Many of the collections that are selling right now advertise only on Google Shopping and YouTube. We track both, so you don’t miss them.

How are collections validated?

Only collections that competitors are already paying to advertise, with demand checked market by market, from generic stores only. Every collection is checked again the next day before it reaches you.

How many collections do I receive?

Normally 1 to 4 a day, one per niche whenever possible; women’s fashion usually gets two. The number depends on what passes validation, and collections still being observed are labelled as such.

Which countries and niches are covered?

The United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Germany, Denmark, France, Italy and Spain. Three niches: women’s fashion, men’s fashion and home decoration.

What is included in the Launch Kit?

A creative prompt tailored to the collection — paste it into ChatGPT or your image AI and generate as many ad creatives as you want — plus reference photos and the collection report. The photos are references only and must not be published in ads.

Do you run my campaigns or produce my creatives?

No. The Launch Kit gives you the reference photos, the creative prompt and the collection report; you generate the creatives with your own AI tools and run your own campaigns. Results depend on your products, pricing, store and campaign management.

Can I cancel?

Yes, whenever you want, in your member area (“Manage subscription”). There is no minimum term.

How much does it cost?

€15 a month, plus a €25 joining fee charged with your first payment. The Click plan is €15 a month with no joining fee.
