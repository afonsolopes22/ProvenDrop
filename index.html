Questions

What exactly is ProvenDrop?

An ecommerce market intelligence service with three components: product research and market validation, AI-powered creative production, and advertising campaign preparation. We discover collections from stores already advertising in international markets, validate them, and deliver each approved one as a Launch Kit. It is not a store, a product catalogue or an advertising agency.

What is the difference between ProvenDrop via Click and ProvenDrop Direct?

Both give access to the same ProvenDrop research and creative resources. Via Click, you join through the Click Dynamics partnership product and receive everything in the WhatsApp channel managed by Click, with its bot and community. Direct, you join on this website, are onboarded and managed by ProvenDrop, and communicate with us directly. Each option has its own Stripe product.

How are collections validated?

Each candidate is scored on 13 metrics across advertising evidence, search demand, store and collection quality, and risk. Brands are excluded. We then measure it again 24 hours later and downgrade it if the advertising falls or the link stops working.

How many collections do I receive?

Normally 1 to 4 a day. The number depends on what passes validation, and collections still being observed are labelled as such.

Which countries and niches are covered?

Italy, Spain, France, Germany, the United Kingdom and the United States. Four niches: women’s jewellery, home decoration, women’s fashion and men’s fashion.

What is included in the Launch Kit?

A one-page guide, reference photographs, prompts for 20 advertising images, prompts for 4 UGC videos, Google Ads keywords, negative keywords, Shopping titles, landing-page copy and the complete opportunity analysis. The photographs are references only and must not be published in ads.

Do you run my campaigns or produce my creatives?

No. The Launch Kit gives you the prompts, references and Google Ads material; you generate the images and videos with your own AI tools and run your own campaigns. Results depend on your products, pricing, store and campaign management.

Is there a member platform?

A private platform is in development. Direct members receive access when it becomes available; until then, research and Launch Kits are delivered to them directly by ProvenDrop. Members via Click continue to use the Click WhatsApp channel.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, whenever you want, with no minimum term. For Direct members, the welcome page you see after paying explains how to manage your subscription. Members via Click follow the model agreed with Click Dynamics.

Why are memberships limited?

To reduce saturation. If too many stores in one country launch the same collection at the same time, it becomes less useful to all of them.
