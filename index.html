Questions

What exactly is ProvenDrop?

An ecommerce market intelligence service. We research collections from stores already advertising in international markets, validate them and send you the results on WhatsApp. It is not a store, a product catalogue or a dashboard.

How are collections validated?

Each candidate is scored on 13 metrics across advertising evidence, search demand, store and collection quality, and risk. Brands are excluded. We then measure it again 24 hours later and downgrade it if the advertising falls or the link stops working.

How many collections do I receive?

Normally 1 to 4 a day. The number depends on what passes validation, and collections still being observed are labelled as such.

Which countries are covered?

Italy, Spain, France, Germany, the United Kingdom and the United States.

Which niches are available?

Women’s jewellery, home decoration, women’s fashion and men’s fashion.

What is included in the ZIP?

A one-page guide, reference photographs, instructions for 20 advertising images, prompts for 4 UGC videos, Google Ads keywords, negative keywords, Shopping titles, landing-page copy and the complete analysis. The photographs are references only and must not be published in ads.

How does WhatsApp delivery work?

The list arrives at 9 AM with each collection’s evidence and kit. At any time you can send a command to filter by niche or country.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, whenever you want, with no minimum term. The welcome page you see after paying explains how to manage your subscription.

Why are memberships limited?

To reduce saturation. If too many stores in one country launch the same collection at the same time, it becomes less useful to all of them.
