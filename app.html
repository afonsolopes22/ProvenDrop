Welcome. One minute to get started.

  1. 1Open a collection“Today” has the day’s collections with the proof for each one: Meta and Google ads, days, reach and countries. Open the collection link to see the competitor store.
  2. 2Download the Launch KitEvery collection in the list comes with a kit: reference photos, a creative prompt tailored to the collection, a video prompt and the collection report. Collections under observation are labelled: test them with a small budget. Read README.txt first.
  3. 3Paste the prompts into your AI toolsThe creative prompt is tailored to each collection — paste it into ChatGPT or your image AI and generate as many ad creatives as you want. The video prompt goes into your video AI, with one of the photos. Attach the reference photos; never publish the originals.
