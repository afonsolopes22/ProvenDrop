Payment received. Welcome to ProvenDrop.

You joined ProvenDrop directly. Your membership is managed by ProvenDrop, and your onboarding happens with us, not through a third-party channel. Here is what happens next.

  1. Check the inbox you paid with

    Stripe has sent your receipt to the e-mail address you used at checkout. That address identifies your membership: every message from ProvenDrop about your membership goes there.

  2. Confirm your niche and your markets

    ProvenDrop will contact you at that address to confirm your niche (women’s jewellery, home decoration, women’s or men’s fashion) and the countries you sell in, and to complete your onboarding. You can also write first:

    Write to ProvenDrop

    Memberships are limited by niche and country. If your combination is already full, we offer you another one or refund your payment.

  3. Receive your research and Launch Kits

    Approved collections and their Launch Kits are delivered to you directly by ProvenDrop: the collection link, its evidence and score, and the kit with image prompts, UGC video prompts, visual references and the Google Ads material.

    A private member platform is in development. As a Direct member you receive access when it becomes available. Nothing else is needed from you until then.

  4. Launch a collection in 1–2 days

    • Choose an approved collection in your niche. The country next to its name is where it is performing: start there.
    • Find the products with your supplier using the reference photographs in the kit (folder 1 - Imagens).
    • Create the images: paste the text from Colar no ChatGPT.txt into your image model together with the photographs, and generate the 20 advertising images.
    • Create the videos with the folder 2 - Videos: a photograph and a ready prompt for each of the 4 UGC videos.
    • Google Ads: the file 3 - Google Ads.txt brings the keywords, negative keywords, Shopping titles and landing-page copy for that country.
    • Read LEIA-ME.txt first: it explains everything on one page.

    Kit documents are currently written in Portuguese.

Rules that protect you

  • The photographs in the kit belong to competitors and are for reference only. Never publish them in your ads.
  • Do not copy competitors’ ad copy: use it as inspiration.
  • No fake discounts, countdowns, invented “stock running out” or health claims. Meta and Google block accounts for this.
  • Shipping in 11–15 days? Seasonal collections (Christmas, Mother’s Day…) launch at least 4 weeks ahead.
